Problem: the contacts picker reported "missing the following permission in AndroidManifest.xml: android.permission.WRITE_CONTACTS" — the Android permission prompt appeared but no names ever came back.
Location: app/android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml
Action: tapping "from the phone book" while adding a supplier.
Cause: @capacitor-community/contacts declares READ_CONTACTS and WRITE_CONTACTS together under one alias ("contacts"), and Capacitor refuses the whole request — pickContact included — if any permission in the alias group is missing from the app manifest. The plugin ships an empty manifest, so the app must declare both. Only READ was declared.
Fix: declare WRITE_CONTACTS as well. Nothing in the app writes to the phone book; the declaration exists only to satisfy the alias group. Verified inside the built APK by reading its binary manifest.
